# Menu audio

`last_light.ogg` is the existing menu music. The thirteen original PCM sound effects are generated by `tools/audio/compose_menu_sfx.py` using NumPy, without recordings or third-party samples: rifle, hammer, footstep, groan, body fall, fence break, timber, campfire, explosion, launcher, swing, grenade, and reload.

Effects use mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit WAV files. `menu_demo_audio.gd` routes positional effects to the SFX bus, with ten reusable voices, per-event rate limits, and one quiet campfire loop. Music retains its separate bus. Reduce Motion and scene teardown stop the demo sounds. Rendered tests capture real audio output and verify SFX muting and cleanup.
